# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Researchers and learners interested in the topics covered by the blog. They visit to find and read relevant posts and to learn about the author's work.

## Product Purpose

The blog publishes paper analysis, engineering practice, and reading notes. It helps visitors explore topics they care about and understand the author's work through those posts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing collections (合集), tags, and categories.
- Preserve the full content of every existing blog post.
